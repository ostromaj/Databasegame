Who wants to beNormalized?

Fifteen tables stand between you and a million dollars. Spot whether each one is in 1NF, 2NF or 3NF, or not even in 1NF. Questions get harder as the money goes up.

The three rules

1NF
Every cell holds one single value. No lists in a cell, no repeating columns like Item1, Item2, Item3. Rows are identified by a primary key.
2NF
1NF, plus no partial dependencies: no non-key column can depend on only part of a composite key. A one-column key gets this for free.
3NF
2NF, plus no transitive dependencies: no non-key column can depend on another non-key column.

Every non-key column depends on the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key.
